Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes"**
($5, top 10 paid, 1st $1.54, deadline 26 Nov 2026 14:40 UTC).

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network, deterministic) writes one line — 89
characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> HR said to bring my whole self to work, so I brought the part that googles jobs at lunch.

The line is judged pairwise by the house judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) against
the published brief, in order: (1) a genuine one-line office joke, (2) a real
turn/surprise at the end, (3) recognition of office truth, (4) tight wording,
(5) freshness (not the obvious Monday morning / photocopier line). The joke is
built for criterion 2: the punchline ("googles jobs at lunch") introduces
entirely new information the setup does not telegraph, a literal-compliance
reveal of the HR cliché "bring your whole self to work".
